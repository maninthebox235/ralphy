# 📱 Mobile Setup Guide - DAX Co-Pilot Helper

## Quick Setup (5 minutes)

### For iPhone/iPad Users

1. **Open the file on your iPhone**
   - Email yourself the `dax-copilot-helper.html` file OR
   - Upload it to iCloud Drive/Dropbox and open from there OR
   - Have it hosted on a simple web server (easiest)

2. **Add to Home Screen**
   - Tap the **Share button** (box with arrow pointing up) at the bottom
   - Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
   - Name it "DAX Helper" or whatever you prefer
   - Tap **"Add"**

3. **Now it works like an app!**
   - Find the icon on your home screen
   - Opens full-screen without Safari UI
   - Works completely offline
   - Fast access during patient visits

### For Android Users

1. **Open the file on your Android phone**
   - Email yourself the file or access via cloud storage
   - Open in Chrome browser

2. **Install as App**
   - You'll see an **"Install"** banner at the top
   - Tap **"Install"** 
   - OR tap the menu (⋮) → "Add to Home Screen"

3. **Access like a native app**
   - Icon appears on your home screen
   - Opens in full-screen mode
   - Works offline

## 🎯 Using on Mobile During Patient Visits

### Bottom Navigation Bar (Auto-appears on phones)
When you open the app on mobile, you'll see 4 quick-access buttons at the bottom:

- **📋 Phrases** - Jump to essential command phrases
- **🏥 Templates** - Access oncology-specific templates
- **⚡ Quick Ref** - Go to quick reference card
- **☰ Menu** - Open full navigation menu

### Search Button (Floating)
- The **🔍 button** floats in the bottom-right corner
- Tap it to quickly search for any phrase or topic
- Perfect for finding specific templates fast

### Best Practices

1. **Before your clinic day:**
   - Open the app and favorite your most-used sections (tap ⭐)
   - Your favorites appear at the top for instant access

2. **During patient visits:**
   - Use the bottom navigation for quick jumps
   - Tap any template box's "Copy" button to copy to clipboard
   - Paste directly into DAX or your notes

3. **Between patients:**
   - Search for specific terms you need
   - All sections are collapsible - tap to expand/collapse

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### "Add to Home Screen" not showing (iPhone)
- Make sure you're in Safari (not Chrome)
- Tap the Share button (not the menu)
- Scroll down in the share menu

### Copy buttons not working
- Grant clipboard permissions when prompted
- Some browsers need manual paste (that's normal)

### Page not loading offline
- Open once while online first
- This caches the page for offline use

### Layout looks weird
- Make sure you're opening the latest version
- Try clearing browser cache

### Search not finding results
- Search is case-insensitive
- Try broader terms
- Make sure sections are expanded
